Welcome to Nadaf Pinjar Sangha

Unity | Education | Empowerment

Working for Social, Educational and Economic Development of the Community

Karnataka State Nadaf / Pinjar Sangha (R), Shivamogga is a registered community organization dedicated to the social, educational, and economic upliftment of the Nadaf/Pinjar community across Karnataka.

Established with a vision of unity and progress, the Sangha operates through a well-structured governing body comprising 95 members (Term 2024–2029), including State Office Bearers, Executive Committee Members, District Presidents, and Women District Presidents.

We strive to build a progressive, educated, and empowered community through organized leadership and collective participation.
